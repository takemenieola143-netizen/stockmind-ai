How the ranking works

Each screen requires price ≥ ₹20, technical score ≥ 65, RSI 45–70, close above SMA50, minimum turnover and maximum ATR risk. Weights are fixed heuristics displayed on each card, not probability of success. Long-term requires independently verified financials and valuation; intraday needs a current-session quote, VWAP, spread and news checks. These are missing. The same stock can qualify for multiple screens. No missing data is replaced with invented evidence.

We screen the latest available completed weekday snapshot, not a verified exchange holiday calendar. When a snapshot is older than expected, it is clearly historical. Daily refresh depends on the scheduled scanner publishing a valid report; weekends, exchange holidays, source restrictions and failed scans may delay it.
